Privacy Policy

Effective Date: 05/11/2024

At Secure Lua, we take your privacy seriously. This Privacy Policy describes how we collect, use, and protect your personal information when you use our website and services. By using Secure Lua, you agree to the terms outlined in this Privacy Policy. Please read this policy carefully to understand how we collect, store, and process your data.

1. Information We Collect

When you register for an account or use the services offered by Secure Lua, we may collect the following information:

1.1 Personal Information

  • Email Address: When you create an account, we require a valid email address to send account-related information, service updates, and other important communications.
  • Password: During registration, you will be asked to create a password. This password is stored in a hashed format to ensure its security. We never store your plain-text password.

1.2 User-Generated Content

  • Projects and Loaders: As a registered user, you can create projects on our platform. Each project generates a unique Lua loader script, which you paste at the top of your Lua scripts to protect them using our whitelist system. The loader checks the provided script_key and your HWID to ensure the script is being used by the authorized user.
  • Script Keys: Every loader is associated with a unique script_key, used to verify the authenticity of the script and ensure it's linked to the correct user and hardware.

1.3 Automatically Collected Information

  • IP Address: Used for security purposes, such as detecting and preventing abuse or fraud.
  • Browser and Device Information: Includes details such as your browser type, operating system, and device information.
  • Cookies: We use cookies to enhance your experience, track usage patterns, and manage your sessions on our website.

2. How We Use Your Information

We use the information we collect for the following purposes:

2.1 Account and Service Management

  • To create and manage your account, including registration, login, and password management.
  • To provide the loader generation service, which allows you to create unique Lua loaders tied to your projects.
  • To manage and store your projects, including the associated Lua scripts and loader scripts.

2.2 Whitelist System

The primary purpose of the loader scripts and associated data is to provide a whitelist system to prevent piracy. When you create a project, we generate a unique loader script, which verifies the script_key and your HWID to ensure only authorized users can run the scripts.

2.3 Communication

We use your email address to communicate with you about your account, important updates, and service notifications, such as:

  • Security alerts
  • Updates or changes to the service
  • Promotional or feature announcements (if applicable)

2.4 Analytics and Improvements

We may use the information we collect to:

  • Analyze how users interact with the service to improve functionality and user experience.
  • Monitor the performance and security of our services.
  • Track and prevent abuse or fraudulent activity.

3. Data Security

We implement strict security measures to protect your personal information from unauthorized access, alteration, or deletion:

  • Encrypted Storage: Your password is securely hashed using industry-standard encryption methods, and we never store it in plain text.
  • Access Control: We limit access to your data to authorized personnel only and use strong security protocols.
  • Secure Transmission: We use HTTPS to encrypt all data transferred between your device and our servers.

However, please note that while we take significant measures to protect your information, no method of electronic storage or transmission over the internet is 100% secure. We cannot guarantee absolute security.
